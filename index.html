Our Story

JAMAL DEV STUDIO was founded by Lawani Djamiou Alade with a singular vision: to make world-class software engineering accessible to businesses of all sizes.

What started as a one-person operation has grown into a full-service digital studio, delivering websites, mobile apps, custom software, and automation solutions to clients across multiple industries.

Our approach combines technical excellence with deep business understanding. We don't just write code — we craft solutions that drive real results, from increased revenue to streamlined operations.

Every project we take on is treated as a partnership. We work closely with our clients to understand their goals, challenges, and vision — then we bring it all to life with precision and creativity.
